Supplied by the user, 22 Aug 2026. Hand-compiled, not extracted — treat as
authoritative for Group I Mains recurrence, the way
`appsc-group2-prep/references/pyq-bank.md` is authoritative for Group II.

## How to seed this without losing 80% of it

The Group II bank was seeded three times before it was right, and every failure
was silent. The same traps apply here:

- **Every `##` heading names BOTH the year and the paper.** The G2 seeder took
  paper identity from the H1 alone, so with `UNIQUE (paper_id, q_no)` and
  `INSERT OR REPLACE`, each paper's Q1 overwrote the last and 1,127 rows
  collapsed to ~150 — while reporting success. Four years x five papers here
  means four Q1(a)s per paper number. Take identity from the `##` line.
- **Questions carry internal choice.** `1 (a) ... OR (b) ...` is ONE question
  slot offering two alternatives. Both halves are evidence of what was asked;
  neither is a separate question number. Store them as (a)/(b) under one q_no.
- **Section headings are `###` and repeat across papers.** "Section I" means
  something different in every paper. Never key on it alone.
- **The syllabus changed between 2017 and 2020.** Paper numbers are NOT
  comparable across that boundary — see below. Recurrence counted naively across
  all four years will attribute 2017 Paper III (Indian Economy) questions to the
  modern Paper III (Polity, Ethics). Map by SUBJECT, not paper number.

## Syllabus restructuring

| Paper | 2017 | 2020 · 2023 · 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| I | General Essay | General Essay |
| II | History & Indian Constitution | History, Culture & Geography of India & AP |
| III | Indian Economy & AP Economy | Polity, Constitution, Governance, Law & Ethics |
| IV | Science & Technology | Economy & Development of India & AP |
| V | Data Appreciation & Interpretation | Science, Technology & Environmental Issues |

All Mains papers: 3 hours, 150 marks. In the 2020–2025 pattern, Papers II–V are
15 questions of 10 marks each with internal choice; Paper I is three essays of
50 marks, one compulsorily from each section.
